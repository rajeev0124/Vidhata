05 Safety I.V. Cannula (Adva Series)

Specially designed safety I.V. cannulas featuring our self-activating needle shield technology. Engineered to drastically lower needle-stick injury rates for frontline clinical personnel.

Polywin Safety Cannula

Ref Code: 1101-1104 / Adva

Eliminates accidental needle exposure upon needle extraction.

  • Self-Activating Guard: Automatic shield deploys over the needle tip upon withdrawal, locking permanently.
  • Adva Flashback Chamber: Transparent barrel provides instant visual verification of successful vein entry.
  • Tapered FEP Catheter: Medical-grade, kink-resistant catheter with ultra-smooth surface properties.
  • integrated Port & Wings: Snap-fit color coded wings for sterile site fixation.
Clinical Standard

Fully compliant with global safety directives. Color-coded according to ISO standards from 14G (Orange) to 26G (Violet) for clear, instantaneous gauge identification.

07 Hemodialysis Catheter & Access Kits

Premium vascular access catheters and dialysis components designed to withstand high flow rates during renal therapies, minimizing vessel friction and thrombotic risks.

Haemocent Catheter Kit

Ref Code: 11110 / Haemocent

Designed to maintain vascular integrity and ensure high flow rates.

  • Biocompatible Polyurethane: Thermosensitive catheter body softens inside the vessel for safety.
  • Coaxial Lumen: Direct dual flow with low recirculation rate.
  • Flexible J-Tip Guidewire: Facilitates easy, atraumatic wire passage.
  • Complete Insertion Set: Includes dilator, valved introducer syringe, and scalpel.
Renal Product Note

Available in single, double, and triple lumen configurations. Features rotatable suture wing tabs to facilitate easy anchoring and secure site fixation.
