Core Capability

Contamination-Free Medical Moulding Excellence

Vidhata Plastics operates state-of-the-art cleanroom injection moulding facilities engineered specifically for critical healthcare applications. Our production lines feature all-electric and high-precision servo moulding presses positioned beneath HEPA laminar flow hoods within certified ISO Class 7 (Class 10,000) and ISO Class 8 (Class 100,000) cleanrooms.

From fluid management components and dialysis accessories to oncology infusion sets and catheter connectors, every part is moulded, robotically extracted, and packaged under strict airborne particulate and bioburden controls.
