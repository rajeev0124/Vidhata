End-to-End Quality Monitoring Systems

Our quality checks are integrated at every key stage of production, from raw polymer verification to final packaging leak testing.

1
Project Lifecycle Monitoring

Stage-gate approval during mould fabrication to ensure alignment with engineering models.

2
Material Verification & COA Analysis

Raw plastics are verified against supplier Certificate of Analysis (COA) records before batch extrusion.

3
Product Stability Testing

Environmental stress cracking and material degradation trials conducted under simulated operational limits.
