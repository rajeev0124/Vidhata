If you are reading this from outside India

Form MD-5 is the manufacturing licence issued by an Indian state licensing authority under the Medical Device Rules 2017. Class B is the second of four risk classes, broadly comparable to Class IIa under the EU MDR and Class II under the US FDA. The comparison is indicative, not a formal equivalence.

CDSCO is India's central drug and device regulator. Our licence and its twenty-six devices are listed on its public register, so you can check every claim on this page without contacting us.
