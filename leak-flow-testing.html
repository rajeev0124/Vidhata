Leak Verification

Fluid Path & Leak Testing

For medical disposables, IV sets, and catheter connectors, ensuring complete leakage prevention and clear flow path is critical. Vidhata Plastics integrates simple, reliable leak testing stations inside our controlled rooms to test components before final packaging.

We perform pressure testing using dry pneumatic air lines, verifying that seals are tight and that there are no blockage defects (occlusions) inside the tubing channels, giving our clients total confidence in product quality.

Quality Verification Parameters

Pressure Decay Testing

Pneumatic pressure decay checks to confirm air-tight seals across connectors, joints, and hubs.

⚙

Flow Path Checking

Flow resistance checks to ensure that the interior tubing channels are fully open and clear of blocks.

🔍

Cleanroom Suite

All testing is performed inside dust-free controlled cleanrooms to prevent biological and particulate contamination.

👁

Visual Inspections

Manual optical inspection under magnification lamps to ensure bubble-free joints and clean components.
