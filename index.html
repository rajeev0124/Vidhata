R&D · Design · Tooling · Manufacture · Turnkey

Join us at any stage. We can take it the rest of the way.

Ten stages under one roof and one quality system. Most partners join at three or four. Some hand us a napkin sketch and take delivery of a registered, packaged product.

Stages 1–3

Develop

  • Ideation and feasibility
  • Product design, DFM and material selection
  • Mould-flow simulation and structural analysis
Stages 4–5

Tool

  • Prototype and rapid tooling
  • Hard tool build, trial and proving
Stages 6–8

Make

  • Validation and specification testing
  • Cleanroom moulding, assembly and leak-test — ISO 14644-1 Class 7 and 8
  • Sterilisation and primary packaging
Stages 9–10

Release

  • QMS, technical file and regulatory support
  • Batch release, logistics and turnkey supply

✓ Worldwide Export Cleared
Next

Regulated export markets

We do not hold free-sale certificates, CE marking or FDA registration yet, so we cannot ship finished devices into the EU or the US today. Free-sale certificates come first.

If you are planning a programme for a regulated market, talk to us anyway — tooling and validation take longer than certification, and both can run in parallel.
