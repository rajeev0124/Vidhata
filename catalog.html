01. Engineering & Toolroom Capabilities

Our state-of-the-art in-house Tool Room transforms ideas into highly precise moulds. By integrating engineering, design, and manufacturing under one roof, we eliminate intermediate supply chain delays and enforce strict tolerances.

Design & DFM

  • CAD/CAM Modeling: 3D product design, reverse engineering, and prototyping using industry-leading platforms.
  • DFM & Mold Flow Analysis: Early optimization of gate locations, wall thickness, cooling, and sink mark prevention.

Tool Manufacturing

  • Mould Types: Multi-cavity injection moulds, extrusion dies, polyurethane (PU) casting moulds, prototype tools.
  • Precision Machining: Ultra-fine tolerances down to ±5 microns for critical medical and electronic parts.

02. Advanced Injection Moulding & Extrusion

We operate high-volume, automated production lines capable of processing a wide range of engineering plastics. Our machinery is configured for high repeatability, minimized waste, and fast cycle times.

Injection Moulding

  • Capacity: Range of Haitian injection moulding machines from 120T to 250T clamping force.
  • Blow Moulding: 60T dedicated Injection Blow Moulding (IBM) line for containers and closures.

Extrusion & PU processing

  • Extrusion Lines: Polycarbonate (PC) and medical grade tubing extrusion with precise dimensional control.
  • Polyurethane Systems: DESMA processing systems and BASF Elastogran systems.

03. ISO 13485 Cleanroom Medical Manufacturing

We operate ISO Class 8 (100,000) cleanroom facilities strictly monitored for dust, temperature, and microbiological contamination. Our processes conform to medical device manufacturing standards.

Cleanroom Moulding

  • Sterile Injection Moulding: Low-particulate, contamination-free moulding of critical fluid pathways.
  • Medical Materials: Certified processing of USP Class VI, medical-grade polycarbonate, acrylic, and PP.

Medical Product Segments

  • Dialysis Components: Dialysis housing elements, blood ports, and end-caps.
  • Fluid Management: I.V. cannula components, catheter manifolds, drip chambers, luer adapters, and closures.

04. Assembly, Packaging & Secondary Services

Vidhata is more than a moulder. We offer extensive post-moulding capabilities including multi-part mechanical assembly, leak testing, medical-grade packaging, and supply chain management.

Assembly Services

  • Mechanical & Manual Assembly: Cleanroom lines for complex disposable devices and electronic housings.
  • Bonding & Welding: Ultrasonic welding, solvent bonding, and gluing for airtight, high-strength joints.

Packaging & Logistics

  • Sterile Packaging: Automated pouch sealing, Tyvek packing, and blister packaging inside cleanrooms.
  • Sterilization Support: Coordination of EO (Ethylene Oxide) or Gamma sterilization protocols.

Quality is built into every stage of our project lifecycle. Our state-of-the-art metrology lab and strict quality systems ensure that every batch of parts meets the exact engineering drawing specifications.
