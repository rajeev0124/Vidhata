Company Profile

Vidhata Life (An SBU of Vidhata Plastics) was established to provide high-quality engineering plastic components at a very competitive cost. We match Chinese pricing structures while delivering superior quality and technical support.

Experienced Engineers

Our core asset is our highly trained design and manufacturing engineers. We apply continuous training in Six Sigma guidelines to eliminate molding defects, optimize cycle times, and maintain dimensional tolerances down to ±5 microns.

Production Facilities

Our infrastructure includes over 400,000 sq ft of manufacturing area, with dedicated Class 100,000 and Class 10,000 cleanroom moulding zones. High-speed Haitian machines, CNC toolrooms, and automated material systems support our operations.

Quality Assurance

Vidhata is committed to maintaining a strict Quality Management System (QMS) compliant with ISO 9001:2015 and ISO 13485 (Medical Quality Standard). We practice a PREVENTIVE APPROACH to manufacturing, enforcing raw material verification, moisture control, and inline parameter checks.

Continuous Improvement

Our dedicated engineers focus on continuous cycle-time reductions and process automation. We utilize mold flow simulations early in the design stage to eliminate parting lines, gate flaws, and mechanical weaknesses before building the hard tools.

Tooling Engineering

01. Engineering & Tool Manufacturing

Machinery & Cavity Core Fitting

Our high-precision tool room designs and builds molds for complex components. By matching CAD engineering with high-speed milling, we guarantee consistent tolerances for medical and industrial applications.

Tool Room Core Capabilities

  • Product Optimization: 3D modeling and DFM analysis to minimize raw material usage and cycle time.
  • Multi-Cavity Moulds: Injection mould tools built for long lifecycles (over 1 Million shots).
  • Extrusion Tooling: High-precision dies for medical and industrial tubing profiles.
  • Hard Tool Machining: Hardened steel cavity blocks for long-term wear resistance.

Medical cleanroom

03. Cleanroom Medical Devices

ISO Class 8 sterile manufacturing

Our dedicated cleanroom moulding and assembly facilities support medical, pharmaceutical, and IVD components. We utilize medical-grade resins and maintain biological barrier protocols.

Cleanroom Capabilities

  • Biocompatible Materials: ISO 13485 compliant processing of USP Class VI raw materials.
  • Dialysis Components: Dialysis housing end caps, blood ports, and hollow-fiber casings.
  • IV Cannula parts: Needle hubs, flashback chambers, luer adapters, and closures.
  • Closed Drainage: Medical tubing, catheter mounts, and urine collection components.
