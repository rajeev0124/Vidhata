What We Are Licensed to Make

Twenty-six sterile single-use medical devices across six therapy areas, plus four pharma packaging formats. Every one is tooled, documented and audited here under CDSCO Form MD-5 licence MFG/MD/2026/000078.

Available under our own VIREXA brand, or made under your brand on the same line — as OEM contract manufacture under our licence, or under your own licence at our premises.
