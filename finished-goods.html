Finished Goods Warehousing & BMR Compliance

For clinical-grade medical disposables and catheter kits, keeping comprehensive documentation of the production batch is essential. Vidhata Plastics operates a clean, organized storage facility where items are logged and stored.

We maintain a complete **Batch Master Record (BMR)** for every run. This record tracks the plastic resin batch, cleanroom assembly date, sealing test outcomes, and final QC signatures, verifying full traceability for our healthcare customers.
