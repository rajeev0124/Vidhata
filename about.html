About Vidhata Life

A plastics company that went and learned the regulations.

Vidhata Life is the medical device and pharma packaging strategic business unit of Vidhata Plastics India Private Limited. Unit 1 at Cherlapally has run since 1991. The toolroom followed in 2009. Unit 2 at Maheshwaram, where the medical work happens, opened in 2022.

The plastics company is thirty-five years old. The medical business is not. Unit 2 opened in 2022 and the licence was issued in February 2026. We would rather you heard that from us.

What carries across is the part that is hard to buy. A toolroom that has cut 530 moulds. A moulding floor that has run 150 million parts. People who have held tight tolerances for customers who check them.
