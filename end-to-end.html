R&D · Design · Tooling · Manufacture · Turnkey

Ten stages, one roof,
one quality system.

Join at any stage. We can take it the rest of the way — or hand it back to you at the end of any one.

Most manufacturers do one or two of these well and subcontract the rest. We own all ten, which is why design intent survives from the first CAD model to the sealed pack, and why there is one company to hold accountable when something is wrong.

01 Develop (01–03) → 02 Tool (04–05) → 03 Make (06–08) → 04 Release (09–10)
Phase 01

Develop

Stages 01 – 03 · Engineering & Design Proving
01 Develop

Ideation and feasibility

Bring a clinical need, a competitor sample or a sketch. We assess whether it can be moulded, what it would cost at volume, and which regulatory route it falls under — before anyone spends money on tooling.

You receive
A feasibility note and an indicative cost per part.
02 Develop

Product design and design for manufacture

Full 3D CAD, part and assembly design, material selection across medical-grade polymers, and design-for-manufacture review. Where a design already exists, we review it for mouldability and cost before we quote.

You receive
A DFM report and a released 3D model.
03 Develop

Simulation and analysis

Mould-flow simulation before a tool is cut, and structural analysis with strength and fatigue assessment where the part carries load. Our engineering lead came from aerospace structures and built this discipline into the company.

You receive
Mould-flow and analysis reports.
Phase 02

Tool

Stages 04 – 05 · Tooling & Mould Proving
04 Tool

Prototype and rapid tooling

Prototype parts and soft tools for design proving, plus rapid tooling in polyurethane — a formulation and processing capability our managing director developed over thirty years.

You receive
Prototype parts you can hold and test.
05 Tool

Hard tool build, trial and proving

The production mould is designed and cut in our own toolroom — 530 moulds and more than 6,000 cavities to date — then trialled and dimensionally proven before it runs. The company that cuts the tool is the company that moulds the part.

You receive
Trial and dimensional reports.
Phase 03

Make

Stages 06 – 08 · Manufacture & Packaging
06 Make

Validation and specification testing

Materials and finished parts tested against the agreed specification in our own QC and stability laboratory, with the quality and regulatory documentation written as we go.

You receive
A validation report.
07 Make

Cleanroom manufacture and assembly

Moulding, assembly and leak-testing inside a 7,263 sq ft cleanroom held at ISO Class 7 and 8 and classified in accordance with ISO 14644-1, with staged in-process quality control at every step.

You receive
In-process QC records.
08 Make

Sterilisation and primary packaging

Sterilisation and primary and secondary packaging on site, so the part never leaves controlled conditions between moulding and the sealed pack.

You receive
The batch record.
Phase 04

Release

Stages 09 – 10 · Compliance & Turnkey Supply
09 Release

QMS, technical file and regulatory support

Device Master Record, Design History File and technical file preparation, and support through the CDSCO route. Delivered alongside a manufacturing programme, with retained regulatory partners where a submission needs specialist work.

You receive
A technical file your regulatory team can submit.
10 Release

Batch release, logistics and turnkey supply

Released against the batch record and shipped with its inspection reports. For turnkey programmes we hold the licence, carry the stock and deliver a finished, packaged, labelled product ready for your channel.

You receive
The release record, and the goods.
